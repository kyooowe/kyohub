> **A little more organized, a little less to remember.**

Kyohub is a family space for keeping everyday plans together—from grocery runs and monthly bills to baby milestones and household logins.

## Made for family life 💙

*One cozy space for the little things that keep your family organized.*

<details>
<summary>👶 <strong>Baby tracker</strong></summary>

<br>

All-in-one daily nursery and infant care tracking for parents.

- **Milk feedings:** Quick-log bottle intake, formula or breast milk, leftover ounces, and exact timestamps.
- **Diapers & stool:** Record wet, dirty, or combined changes with consistency and stool color guides.
- **Sleep sessions:** Track naps and night sleep with a live timer, awake duration, and daily rest breakdowns.
- **Growth progression:** Monitor monthly height, weight, and head circumference percentiles against WHO benchmarks.
- **Immunizations & meds:** Keep vaccine schedules organized and track daily medication doses.
- **Pediatrician notes:** Save doctor visit logs, advice, prescriptions, and symptoms.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🛒 <strong>Grocery planner</strong></summary>

<br>

Your family's shopping list, all in one place.

- Organize shopping lists by date.
- Check off items as you shop.
- Revisit lists whenever you need them.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🗓️ <strong>Family calendar</strong></summary>

<br>

Keep track of the family events and milestones that matter.

- Schedule vaccinations and important dates.
- Add notes for upcoming appointments.
- Track completed needs and activities.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💰 <strong>Budget planner</strong></summary>

<br>

Make everyday family finances easier to manage.

- Set monthly budgets.
- Track recurring and one-time bills.
- Review expenses across six months.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🔐 <strong>Password manager</strong></summary>

<br>

Keep your family's shared logins organized and easy to find.

## Get started

Deploying your own private family hub is completely free and takes under 15 minutes! We built an interactive, beginner-friendly deployment guide directly into KyoHub with step-by-step checklists, an interactive `.env` builder, and copy-paste commands:

👉 **[How to Deploy KyoHub on Your Own](https://kyohub-one.vercel.app/deploy)**

### 🚀 What the tutorial covers:

1. **Database Setup:** Create a free PostgreSQL cloud database with Prisma in a few clicks.
2. **Project Setup:** Download Node.js & VS Code, clone KyoHub, and sync your `.env` credentials.
3. **Vercel Deployment:** Deploy your private hub live to the cloud with your own 24/7 accessible URL.

> 💡 **Beginner Friendly:** No coding or technical experience required! The tutorial includes everyday analogies, a beginner glossary popup, and troubleshooting guides for common deployment questions.

## Built with

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 and React 19
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Data:** Prisma ORM and PostgreSQL
- **UI:** Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui, and Base UI
- **Charts and icons:** Recharts and Lucide React
- **Reminders:** Web Push

## A note about saved passwords

Saved password values are stored unencrypted in the database. Everyone who can access the app can read them; the PIN gate is not a substitute for secure credential storage. Avoid storing sensitive passwords and keep the app private.
